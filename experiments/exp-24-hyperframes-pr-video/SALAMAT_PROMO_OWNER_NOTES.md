# Salamat Mebel Promo — Owner Notes

Updated: 2026-10-08

These notes are authoritative owner-directed revisions for the active Salamat Mebel promo fixture.

## Mandatory tactile countertop shot

Add a premium tactile kitchen-material shot to the promo.

### Placement
Prefer Scene 5 / materials-quality section, unless the current storyboard has already evolved and a nearby equivalent placement is stronger.

### Visual direction
- slow camera glide along a kitchen countertop;
- close-up / medium-close framing;
- a person's hand slowly runs across the countertop surface;
- the hand should visibly feel / explore the material texture rather than simply rest on it;
- use soft side lighting so the surface texture, edge quality and finish read clearly;
- the shot should communicate tactile quality, craftsmanship and the pleasure of touching good furniture;
- avoid a generic “catalogue product shot” feel;
- keep the movement calm and premium;
- prefer real licensed footage from Storyblocks if a suitable clip exists;
- use generated footage only if no convincing real stock option is available.

### Editorial intent
The viewer should feel that good furniture is not only seen but physically experienced through material, texture and finishing quality.

### Narration fit
Preferred meaning:
> Хорошая мебель ощущается сразу — в фактуре поверхности, в точности деталей и в том, как ею пользуешься каждый день.

Wording may be adjusted for timing and flow, but preserve the tactile-quality idea.

### Minimal-diff rule
Do not restart the production or discard already approved/usable work solely because of this note. Apply the smallest storyboard/media/narration change needed to integrate the shot cleanly.
