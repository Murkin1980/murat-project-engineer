## Hypothesis
The HackerTube video catalog and Hacker News discussion provenance can reveal independently verifiable methods relevant to evidence-first process reconstruction, root-cause analysis, business interviews, provenance graphs and operations research, without building a second crawler or data store.

## Minimal research-only pilot
1. Check accessible published catalogue/export and licensing, without unapproved scraping.
2. Retrieve up to ten exact attributable videos across process mining, systems thinking, customer discovery, knowledge graphs/provenance, theory of constraints.
3. Record links, topic, original discussion, retrieval date, validated claim, provenance and confidence.
4. Compare candidate methods against existing Business Discovery canonical principles; distinguish novelty from duplication.
5. PASS if at least two applicable genuinely new methods or checks are verifiable; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE/REJECT with evidence.
